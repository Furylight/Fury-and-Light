Frequently Asked

How fast is delivery in Sri Lanka?

Within Colombo: 1–2 business days. Outstation & island-wide: 2–4 business days via registered courier.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) island-wide and direct Bank Transfer (Sampath, Commercial, HNB, FriMi).

Can I return or exchange if the size is wrong?

Yes! We offer a smooth 7-day exchange guarantee. Simply contact us on WhatsApp with your unworn garment.

Is there a minimum quantity for custom prints?

No minimums! You can order just 1 single custom printed t-shirt or 100+ pieces for events and clubs.
