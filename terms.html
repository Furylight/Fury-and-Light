RETURNS, TERMS & CARE

Everything you need to know about our 7-day exchange guarantee, delivery timelines, and washing instructions.

7-Day Hassle-Free Exchange Policy

At Fury & Light, customer happiness is our priority. If you receive your t-shirt and find that the size isn't the perfect fit, you can exchange it within 7 days of delivery.

Exchange Eligibility Requirements:
  • Garment must be unworn, unwashed, and odor-free.
  • Original tags and packaging must remain intact.
  • Proof of purchase (order message on WhatsApp or invoice) must be provided.

Manufacturing Defect Guarantee: In the rare event of any sewing fault, fabric rip, or incorrect print color on delivery, we will replace your item immediately with free return shipping.

Custom Order Returns: Custom printed shirts (with customer-provided personal artwork, names or quotes) cannot be exchanged for size error once approved by you on WhatsApp. However, if there is any print defect or damaged fabric, we will happily reprint or refund.

Island-Wide Shipping & Delivery

We ship to every city, district, and town across Sri Lanka using premier courier partners (PromptX, Domex, Certis Lanka, Pronto).

  • Colombo & Suburbs: Delivered within 1 to 2 business days.
  • Outstation Districts: Delivered within 2 to 4 business days.
  • Custom Print Orders: Require 24–48 hours for production before courier dispatch.
  • Free Shipping: Automatically applied to all orders over Rs. 6,000.

A tracking number and SMS notification will be sent to your phone as soon as your package leaves our fulfillment center.

Washing & Print Care Guide

Our high-density DTF and Japanese screen prints are engineered to withstand 60+ machine washes. Follow these simple steps to keep your colors vibrant for years:

Turn Inside Out Always invert the shirt before washing and ironing to protect the graphic print from friction.
Cold Wash (Below 30°C) Use cold or lukewarm water with mild detergent. Avoid harsh bleaching agents or fabric softeners.
Air Dry in Shade Hang to dry in a shaded area. Avoid prolonged exposure to intense direct noon sunlight.
Do NOT Iron on Print Never place a hot iron directly onto the printed graphic. Iron from the inside or cover with cotton cloth.
